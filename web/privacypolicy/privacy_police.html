By installing and playing our Games, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

In all of our Games, we strictly follow a data minimization principle to protect our users:

  • No Account Required: We do not require players to create any account to experience the Games.
  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not actively collect, store, use, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise geographic location).
  • Local Game Data: All game progress, high scores, achievements, and settings are stored directly on your device's local storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Partners

To operate and optimize the gaming experience, our Games may integrate third-party services. These partners may automatically collect certain technical information (such as anonymous advertising IDs, device hardware specifications, and OS version). Depending on the specific Game, these services include:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Helps us collect anonymous analytical data (e.g., game duration, crash logs, popular features) to improve performance and quality.
  • Google AdMob: Provides advertisements within the Games. AdMob may use cookies or unique advertising identifiers on your device to serve ads.

You can review the privacy policies of these third-party partners here:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy Policy

Our Games are designed for a general audience, including children aged 3 and above. We strictly comply with online child protection regulations (such as COPPA and GDPR):

  • We do not knowingly collect or request any personal information from children.
  • For Games designated for children or where children are part of the target audience, we configure third-party services (especially AdMob) to only display non-personalized ads and ensure ad content is thoroughly filtered to be family-appropriate.

4. Data Security

Since your game data is stored locally on your personal device, the safety of this data depends on your device's security. We highly recommend securing your device against unauthorized access.
